Project 02 — MSc group project (PAL) · QMUL · Team leaderIn progress · 2026

Shock to a Company's Equities & Derivatives

How did Exxon Mobil's stock returns and implied volatility respond to escalation and de-escalation events in the 2026 Strait of Hormuz crisis?

FIG. A2 — schematic event window; results in progress
  • As a multinational oil and gas producer, Exxon's revenue is closely tied to the oil price, which surged when the conflict closed the Strait.
  • Using Bloomberg data, we measure Exxon's abnormal returns and changes in its implied volatility surface around each event, and test whether markets reacted differently to escalation than to de-escalation.
  • We also build a C++ program that prices European calls under Black–Scholes and computes implied volatility by bisection.
UNDERLYING
Exxon Mobil (XOM)
METHOD
Event study · IV surface
PRICING ENGINE
C++ · Black–Scholes
WEIGHT
40% × 4 modules
STACK: Bloomberg · C++ · Black–Scholes · Implied volatility (bisection) · Event study · Abnormal returns
